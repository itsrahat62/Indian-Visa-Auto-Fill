How It Works

1
Import

Drop a passport scan or visa PDF. Name, passport number, DOB, and expiry are extracted automatically.

2
Fill Profile

Check the imported details and add what the nine-page form asks for, once.

3
Auto-Fill Portal

Click the floating widget or press Alt+Shift+F on indianvisa-bangladesh.nic.in.

4
Teach Mode

Press Alt+Shift+T to teach custom fields in one click.

Intentionally Manual Safeguards

  • Captcha — A guess is typed in for you; you check it against the picture and press Enter. Nothing is submitted on its own.
  • Declaration Questions — Standard legal declarations remain under your review unless enabled in settings.
  • Final Form Review — Always review entered data before final submission.

Shortcuts
